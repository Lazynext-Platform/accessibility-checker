# Launch Plan: Accessibility Checker Google Ads Campaign
## Objective
Reach web developers and accessibility professionals to promote the Accessibility Checker tool, driving traffic to the deployed site (index.html) and encouraging users to try the client-side version of the product.

## Target Audience
* Web developers
* Accessibility professionals
* Small business owners and solo entrepreneurs responsible for website management

## Keywords
* Accessibility compliance
* Web accessibility tools
* WCAG 2.2 guidelines
* Website accessibility checker
* ADA compliance
* Section 508 compliance

## Ad Groups
### Ad Group 1: Web Accessibility Tools
* Keywords: web accessibility tools, accessibility checker, wcag 2.2 guidelines
* Ad Copy:
	+ Headline: Ensure Website Accessibility with Our AI-Powered Tool
	+ Description: Scan your website for accessibility compliance issues and get recommendations for improvement. Try our client-side Accessibility Checker tool today!
### Ad Group 2: Accessibility Compliance
* Keywords: accessibility compliance, ada compliance, section 508 compliance
* Ad Copy:
	+ Headline: Stay Ahead of Accessibility Regulations with Our Tool
	+ Description: Ensure your website meets accessibility standards with our AI-powered Accessibility Checker. Get started now and avoid costly lawsuits!
### Ad Group 3: Web Development and Accessibility
* Keywords: web development, accessibility professionals, website accessibility
* Ad Copy:
	+ Headline: Build Accessible Websites with Our Expert Tool
	+ Description: As a web developer or accessibility professional, ensure your websites meet accessibility standards with our client-side Accessibility Checker tool. Try it now!

## Landing Page
The deployed site (index.html) will serve as the landing page, providing a working client-side version of the Accessibility Checker tool. Visitors can try the tool directly in the browser, scanning their website for accessibility compliance issues and receiving recommendations for improvement.

## Budget Allocation
* Ad Group 1: Web Accessibility Tools (30%)
* Ad Group 2: Accessibility Compliance (25%)
* Ad Group 3: Web Development and Accessibility (45%)

## Bidding Strategy
* Cost-per-click (CPC) bidding
* Target CPC: $5.00
* Daily budget: $500.00

## Ad Schedule
* Run ads continuously, 24/7

## Location Targeting
* United States
* Canada
* United Kingdom
* Australia
* Germany

## Language Targeting
* English

## Ad Extensions
* Site link extensions: link to the Accessibility Checker tool, features, and pricing pages
* Callout extensions: highlight key benefits, such as "AI-Powered", "Client-Side", and "Easy to Use"

## Conversion Tracking
* Track website conversions, including:
	+ Tool usage (e.g., number of scans, recommendations accepted)
	+ Contact form submissions
	+ Pricing page views

## Monitoring and Optimization
* Monitor ad performance regularly, adjusting ad copy, targeting, and bidding as needed to optimize campaign performance.
* Use Google Ads conversion tracking to measure campaign effectiveness and make data-driven decisions.
