Accessibility Checker Pro License
=====================================

Introduction
------------

The Accessibility Checker Pro license is designed for small businesses and solo entrepreneurs who require advanced features and support to ensure their websites are accessible and compliant with regulations. This document outlines the features and limitations of the Pro license, providing clear explanations and examples to help users understand the benefits and constraints of this license.

Features
--------

The Pro license includes the following features:

1. **Advanced Scanning**: The Pro license allows for advanced scanning of websites, including scanning of password-protected areas, scanning of websites with complex authentication mechanisms, and scanning of websites with a large number of pages.
2. **Customizable Reporting**: Pro license users can customize the reporting features to suit their needs, including the ability to generate reports in various formats, such as PDF, CSV, and Excel.
3. **Priority Support**: Pro license users receive priority support, including access to a dedicated support team, faster response times, and assistance with implementing accessibility recommendations.
4. **Integration with Popular CMS**: The Pro license includes integration with popular content management systems (CMS) such as WordPress, Joomla, and Drupal, making it easy to implement accessibility recommendations.
5. **Accessibility Statement Generator**: The Pro license includes an accessibility statement generator, which helps users create a customized accessibility statement for their website.

Limitations
------------

The Pro license has the following limitations:

1. **Number of Scans**: The Pro license allows for a limited number of scans per month, depending on the plan chosen. Exceeding the limit will result in additional fees.
2. **Number of Pages**: The Pro license has a limit on the number of pages that can be scanned per website. Exceeding the limit will result in additional fees.
3. **Support Response Time**: While Pro license users receive priority support, the response time is still limited to 24-48 hours, depending on the complexity of the issue.
4. **Customization**: While the Pro license allows for some customization, it is still limited to the features and options provided by the Accessibility Checker.

Examples
--------

The following examples illustrate the benefits and limitations of the Pro license:

* **Example 1**: A small business with a simple website and limited budget can use the Pro license to scan their website and receive a customized report. However, if they exceed the limit on the number of scans per month, they will incur additional fees.
* **Example 2**: A solo entrepreneur with a complex website and multiple CMS installations can use the Pro license to integrate the Accessibility Checker with their CMS and receive priority support. However, if they require customization beyond what is provided by the Accessibility Checker, they may need to upgrade to a custom plan.

Pricing
-------

The Pro license pricing is as follows:

* **Monthly Plan**: $99/month (billed annually)
* **Annual Plan**: $999/year (billed annually)
* **Custom Plan**: pricing varies depending on the specific requirements and needs of the user

Conclusion
----------

The Accessibility Checker Pro license is designed to provide small businesses and solo entrepreneurs with advanced features and support to ensure their websites are accessible and compliant with regulations. While the Pro license has limitations, it provides a cost-effective solution for users who require more than the basic features of the Accessibility Checker. By understanding the features and limitations of the Pro license, users can make informed decisions about their accessibility needs and choose the plan that best suits their requirements.
