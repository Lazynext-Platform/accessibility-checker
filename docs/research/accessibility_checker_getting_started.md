Getting Started with Accessibility Checker
==============================================

### Introduction

Welcome to Accessibility Checker, an AI-powered tool that scans small business websites for accessibility compliance issues and provides recommendations for improvement. This guide will walk you through the process of getting started with Accessibility Checker and using its core features to ensure your website is accessible to all users.

### Step 1: Accessing the Tool

To access Accessibility Checker, simply navigate to our website and click on the "Get Started" button. You will be redirected to the tool's interface, where you can begin the onboarding process.

### Step 2: Onboarding Tutorial

Our interactive onboarding tutorial will guide you through the process of using Accessibility Checker. The tutorial consists of the following steps:

1. **Introduction to Accessibility Checker**: Learn about the tool's features and benefits.
2. **Setting up your website**: Enter your website's URL and configure the scanning settings.
3. **Running your first scan**: Initiate a scan of your website and view the results.
4. **Understanding the results**: Learn how to interpret the scan results and identify accessibility issues.
5. **Prioritizing and fixing issues**: Get guidance on how to prioritize and fix accessibility issues.

### Step 3: Configuring Scanning Settings

To configure the scanning settings, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Settings" icon in the top-right corner of the interface.
2. Select the scanning options, such as the types of issues to scan for and the frequency of scans.
3. Save your changes.

### Step 4: Running a Scan

To run a scan of your website, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Scan" button in the top-left corner of the interface.
2. Enter your website's URL and select the scanning options.
3. Click on the "Start Scan" button.

### Step 5: Viewing and Interpreting Results

To view and interpret the scan results, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Results" tab in the interface.
2. View the list of identified accessibility issues, prioritized by severity.
3. Click on an issue to view more details and recommendations for fixing it.
