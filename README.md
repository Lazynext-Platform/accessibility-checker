# Accessibility Checker

Accessibility Checker is an AI-powered tool that scans small business websites for accessibility compliance issues and provides recommendations for improvement.

## Why Accessibility Checker?
As accessibility regulations become more stringent, small businesses need an easy and affordable way to ensure their websites are compliant. Accessibility Checker fills this gap by providing a simple and cost-effective solution for small business owners and solo entrepreneurs.

## Features
* AI-powered scanning for accessibility compliance issues
* Recommendations for improvement
* Easy to use and affordable

## Getting Started
To get started with Accessibility Checker, simply sign up for an account and enter your website's URL. Our AI-powered tool will scan your website and provide a report outlining any accessibility compliance issues and recommendations for improvement.
